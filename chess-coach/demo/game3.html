The one rule from this game

When an attack stalls, the question stops being "what else can I throw?" and becomes "can I repeat?" At moves 23 and 27 the position was binary: Qh7+ and a dead draw, or anything else and you're lost. You had already played that check at move 25, so you knew it was there.

A perpetual isn't giving up. It's the attack collecting what it actually earned — and half a point against a 1400 for an attack that didn't quite land is a good trade.
